A platform for Saudi automotive manufacturing

5-minute read

Connecting the value chain

TASARU Supplier Hub is a dedicated platform for localizing critical automotive manufacturing capabilities in Saudi Arabia. Developed by TASARU Mobility Investments, the hub connects global supplier expertise with the needs of a growing automotive ecosystem.

The platform brings specialist suppliers and the practical foundations of manufacturing together, helping establish critical capabilities closer to Saudi automotive production.

A focused role within MASARAT

Located in MASARAT Mobility Park™ in King Abdullah Economic City, TSH brings the supplier platform into a wider automotive and mobility destination. Its role centers on the practical foundations of manufacturing, including land, real estate and equipment.

This setting connects the hub to the wider automotive environment, supporting the relationship between specialist component capabilities and local manufacturing requirements.

Making localization tangible

The story begins with specialist knowledge and continues through the establishment of local capability. Bringing components closer to Saudi production supports a more connected automotive supply chain.

Across seating, body-in-white, chassis, interior plastics and exterior plastics, the five-supplier platform brings complementary disciplines into one manufacturing environment.

Explore the platform

The foundations behind manufacturing

A manufacturing platform starts with the physical environment in which suppliers work. Land, production space and equipment form a practical foundation, while the relationship between these elements shapes how a facility can support different component categories.

For an automotive supplier, the wider setting matters as much as the individual building. Access, utilities, movement of materials and the arrangement of production areas all contribute to the conversation about establishing local capability.

Bringing complementary disciplines together

Seating, body-in-white, chassis, interior plastics and exterior plastics each involve different processes and specialist knowledge. Bringing these categories into the same platform creates a clear way to explain the breadth of the supplier network without losing sight of each discipline.

The shared platform connects these capabilities through a common focus on local manufacturing. Each supplier retains its own expertise, while the wider setting provides context for how critical components relate to the automotive value chain.

A connected view of localization

Localization can be understood as the connection between knowledge, manufacturing foundations and production requirements. The hub brings these themes together in one story, from the capabilities of global suppliers to the setting in which local manufacturing can take shape.

As readers explore the platform, the supplier profiles and location pages provide different perspectives on that connection. Together, they explain the purpose of TSH and its place within the wider automotive environment.
