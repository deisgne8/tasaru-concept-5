The starting point is expertise

Localization begins with specialist manufacturing knowledge. Global automotive suppliers bring experience that forms the foundation for establishing critical component capabilities.

Putting the foundations in place

Land, manufacturing space and production equipment create the setting in which expertise can become local capability. TSH connects these foundations within a focused supplier platform.

Connecting capability to production

Local manufacturing moves critical components closer to the Kingdom’s growing OEM base. The wider result is a stronger connection across the automotive value chain, with knowledge and capability developing within Saudi Arabia.

Explore the platform

Knowledge becomes a practical process

Manufacturing expertise combines an understanding of materials, equipment, production sequences and the requirements of the finished component. Translating that knowledge into a local setting involves considering how these elements work together within the manufacturing environment.

The process is more than the placement of machinery. It also includes the relationship between people, working areas and the flow of parts, with each discipline contributing its own perspective on how capability is established.

A setting for specialist capability

Production space provides the context in which equipment and knowledge come together. The layout of a facility, its supporting services and the way materials move through it are all part of the broader manufacturing picture.

Within the TSH story, land, real estate and equipment describe these physical foundations. The supplier network adds specialist experience across five component categories, connecting the platform to the practical requirements of automotive manufacturing.

Understanding the wider value chain

Component manufacturing sits within a wider network of activities, from material supply and logistics to vehicle assembly. Bringing specialist capabilities closer to production creates a clearer connection between the individual component and the environment in which it is used.

The journey from expertise to capability can be explored through the platform, supplier and location pages. Each offers a different view of the same ambition: connecting global manufacturing knowledge with local automotive production.
