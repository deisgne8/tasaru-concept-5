King Abdullah Economic City provides the location context for TASARU Supplier Hub, connecting the supplier platform to a wider industrial environment in Saudi Arabia. Within KAEC sits the King Salman Automotive Cluster, home to MASARAT Mobility Park™ and the supplier hub itself. Its proximity to King Abdullah Port links the hub to domestic, regional and international markets.
